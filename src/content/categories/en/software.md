This category covers software used to create, train, inspect, evaluate, or deploy the systems and
projects documented by the wiki. It includes standalone applications and supporting libraries,
regardless of developer or ownership.

The article's subject must be an application, library, framework, protocol, or tool. Developers,
stewards, underlying research methods, and model families remain in their own categories rather than
inheriting Software from related products or implementations.
