This category covers fictional identities and interactive personas. Articles document their roles,
creators, affiliations, appearances, and the projects or settings in which they occur.

Membership is limited to articles whose subject is a character or persona. Frameworks, works,
software, and organizations do not enter this category merely because they describe, feature, or
support characters.
