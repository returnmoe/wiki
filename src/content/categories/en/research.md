This category covers work from the broader research community, including machine-learning
foundations, model training, generative systems, interpretability methods, published studies,
evaluation benchmarks, and reproducible experiments. Research may originate in universities,
companies, nonprofit laboratories, independent teams, or individual researchers; articles identify
authorship and affiliation where applicable.

Membership follows the research subject itself, including research questions, fields, methods,
studies, experiments, benchmarks, models, and purpose-built research software. An organization does
not enter this category solely because it performs or publishes research.
