**Rodrigo Laneth** is a Brazilian security researcher, software developer, and infrastructure
operator. They founded [return moe](/return-moe/) and serve as the studio's lead researcher,
developing its characters, software, and applied artificial intelligence
research.[^personal-site][^return-site]

## Work at return moe

Laneth's work at return moe includes language-model evaluation, mechanistic interpretability,
purpose-specific AI agents, and experiments in generative media.[^return-site][^miru][^benchmark]
They created and originally authored [Soraya](/soraya/), and have developed chatbot systems
used to instantiate the character. Their writing distinguishes Soraya's fictional identity from the
changing prompts, models, and software used to make her interactive.[^soraya-essay]

They introduced [Miru Tracer](/miru-tracer/) under the Miru name as a workbench for inspecting
and intervening in language-model generation. Their other return moe research includes the
[Chinese Political Neutrality Benchmark](/chinese-political-neutrality-benchmark/) and the
[Watermark Removal as a Denoising Task](/watermark-removal-as-a-denoising-task/)
experiment.[^miru][^benchmark][^watermark]

## Visual representation

The infobox portrait depicts an anime-style original character that Laneth generally uses to
represent themself on social media. The character has long, straight dark charcoal hair with loose
bangs, large blue-gray eyes, pale skin, and a restrained smile. She wears a black skirt and blazer
against an abstract blue background.[^return-site]

## Broader work

Laneth is also affiliated with Miralium Research and works in information security and Internet
infrastructure. Their wider biography, security research, infrastructure work, and projects outside
return moe are documented on their official website.[^personal-site][^miralium]
