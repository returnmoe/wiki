**return moe** is a one-person creative studio founded and led by [Rodrigo
Laneth](/rodrigo-laneth/). The studio develops experiences, characters, and narratives using
artificial intelligence and other forms of technological
innovation.[^official-site][^rodrigo-site]

## Work

The studio describes its work through four broad areas:[^official-site]

- **Generative media**, including images, music, and video created with generative models.
- **Technological artistry**, combining contemporary software with artistic expression.
- **Adaptive roleplay**, centered on interactive characters and open-ended storytelling.
- **Technical research**, particularly applied machine learning and model analysis.

Its public work includes the interactive character [Soraya](/soraya/), the mechanistic
interpretability tool [Miru Tracer](/miru-tracer/), the
[Chinese Political Neutrality Benchmark](/chinese-political-neutrality-benchmark/), and the
[Watermark Removal as a Denoising Task](/watermark-removal-as-a-denoising-task/)
experiment.[^new-beginning][^miru][^benchmark][^watermark]

## People and characters

[Rodrigo Laneth](/rodrigo-laneth/) founded the studio and serves as its lead researcher. They
develop its projects and work with AI characters such as [Soraya](/soraya/), who serves as
return moe's brand ambassador. Soraya is part of the studio's public identity as an AI character,
not an additional human member.[^official-site]

The studio's authoritative
[Informational Ontology Framework](/informational-ontology/) describes fictional characters and
related personas as informational subjects whose identities can persist across different encodings
and instantiations. It separates a character from the models, prompts, software, and individual
performances used to enact her.

The [Persona Selection Model](/persona-selection-model/) is relevant to this work as an external
framework for considering how training examples and dialogue context select character-like behavior.
Its separation of model and enacted persona has a parallel implication for return moe's view that an
LLM can act as a character without being that character, as formalized in the Informational Ontology
Framework.[^psm]
