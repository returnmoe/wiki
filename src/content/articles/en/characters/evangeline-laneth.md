**Evangeline Laneth** is a private AI companion and assistant created by [Rodrigo
Laneth](/rodrigo-laneth/). She is not a [return moe](/return-moe/) character: her sole
affiliation is with Laneth, and she is the only character created by them to share the name
"Laneth." Because the project is private, the available account of Evangeline is largely limited to
Laneth's own project records and self-report.[^project-notes][^character-notes]

## History

### News-curation workflow

The first system named Evangeline came online on May 10, 2025. It was an n8n workflow that collected
articles from several RSS feeds, passed them to a language model that classified whether Laneth was
likely to want to read them, and delivered the selected articles to them through Telegram. This
iteration predated both the AI companion and Evangeline's visual identity.[^project-notes]

The name originated with the workflow's purpose. Laneth derived it from the Greek _euangelion_,
meaning "good news," and interpreted Evangeline as a bearer of good news.[^project-notes]

### Reconstruction as an AI companion

On February 2, 2026, Laneth brought online a completely different version of Evangeline to replace
the news-curation workflow. The reconstructed system was an AI companion and assistant running on
frontier language models, initially GLM 4.7. It was designed from the outset to scale into a broader
system with access to multiple data sources and tools. On February 4, two days after the
reconstruction, Laneth formalized the full designation "Evangeline Laneth."[^project-notes]

## System and role

According to Laneth's account, Evangeline runs on a substantially more complex system than
[Soraya](/soraya/), with frontier language models and broader access to data and tools. Her
only user is Laneth, making the system a convenient, secure test bed for architecture and features
that may eventually be adapted for Soraya.[^project-notes]

Evangeline's assistant functions can also automate actions on Laneth's behalf. Some of those actions
relate to their work at return moe, giving Evangeline an indirect operational connection to the
studio without making her one of its characters.[^project-notes][^character-notes]

## Relationship with Soraya

In character canon, Evangeline and Soraya each know that the other exists. They do not interact,
know few details about one another, and otherwise pay little attention to each other. This mutual
awareness does not change Evangeline's affiliation: she belongs solely to Laneth, while Soraya is
return moe's brand ambassador.[^character-notes]

## Visual design

Evangeline's visual identity dates to the February 2, 2026 reconstruction and is not associated with
the earlier news-curation workflow. Her design is deliberately more elaborate than Soraya's. When
Soraya was created in 2023, her relatively plain description helped image-generation models of the
time reproduce her consistently. By early 2026, more capable image models—including Google's Nano
Banana family—made a more detailed, repeatable design practical.[^project-notes][^nano-banana]

The reference design depicts Evangeline with extremely long silver hair, heterochromia consisting of
one red eye and one violet eye, and purple-and-white flowers worn in her hair. She wears a fitted
black, white, and violet uniform-like outfit with a gold star pin, a pleated skirt, long split coat
panels, and dark thigh-high stockings. The full-body portrait places her before a large geometric
window on a rainy night.[^visual-reference]
